05 / METHODOLOGY

How Gallery Management Works

A five-stage curatorial lifecycle connecting artist registration, artwork assignment, and publication.

STAGE 01

Register Artists

Establish biographical records, artistic mediums, nationalities, and historical styles.

STAGE 02

Catalogue Artworks

Record physical dimensions, medium, Indian Rupee valuation, and commercial availability.

STAGE 03

Create Exhibitions

Schedule curated themes and calendar periods with strict overlap conflict detection.

STAGE 04

Allocate Spaces

Assign pieces to rooms with automatic capacity limits and double-booking verification.

STAGE 05

Publish Catalogue

Generate printable wall placards, running sequence numbers, and collector summaries.
